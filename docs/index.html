ClaudeReset

A desk display that shows your Claude quota: an ESP8266 with a round 240×240 panel, fed by a LAN service that signs in to Claude and refreshes the tokens by itself.

The dial shows the 7-day window, or the 5-hour one: an optional button on D0 flips between them, and the choice survives a reboot. The whole map — panel, button and the three-way 3V3 rail — is in docs/wiring.md.
